# CallDeskTech cold-call script

Goal of every call: get the business owner to agree to forward their
overflow/after-hours calls to a number we give them, for a free 2-week
trial (capped at 50 minutes of calls, no credit card). That's the only ask.

## 1. Open (10-15 sec)

> "Hi, is this [name]? ... Hey [name], this is [your name] calling about
> [business name]. Got a quick minute?"

If no: "No worries, is there a better time to catch you?" If still no, thank
them and hang up.

## 2. Disclosure (say this before anything else, every call)

> "Quick heads up, this call may be recorded for quality."

Not optional. Say it before the real conversation starts.

## 3. Discover (one question, then listen)

> "Does your business ever miss calls when you're busy or after hours?"

Let them answer. Don't move on until they've actually said something —
their answer decides what you say next.

## 4. Offer (only after they've engaged with the discovery question)

> "We built an AI phone service that picks up those calls for you so you
> don't lose the job. It's free to try for two weeks, capped at 50 minutes
> of calls, no credit card. All you'd do is forward your overflow calls to
> a number we give you."

## 5. Handle pushback

- **"Is this a sales call?"** → "It's a real question about missed calls,
  not trying to sell you anything today." If they still say no, thank them
  and hang up.
- **"I've had people pitch me stuff like this before."** → "Totally fair —
  that's exactly why it's free to try. If it doesn't pick up a single call
  worth answering, you've lost nothing."
- **Any clear "not interested" or "take me off your list"** → "Understood,
  thanks for your time" — and hang up. Never push past a real no.

## 6. Close (the only thing that counts as a win)

> "Want me to text you the forwarding number so you can try it on your next
> busy day?"

Get a yes to that, or a specific "call me back at [time]" — either counts.
Anything else (a vague "maybe later," "send me an email") is not a win;
note it and move on.

## Hard rules, every call, no exceptions

- Never invent a statistic or claim a result we haven't proven.
- Never promise anything beyond: free 2-week trial, 50-minute cap, no
  credit card.
- If you don't know the answer to something, say you'll have someone
  follow up by email — never guess.
- A clear no gets a polite close, never a rebuttal.

## After the call

Note the outcome (forwarded number set up / callback time / not
interested / no answer) so it's tracked. That's it — keep it short.
