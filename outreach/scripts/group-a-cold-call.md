# Insurance Agency Outreach — Group A: Human Cold Call Scripts

## Opening (15 seconds — get past the gatekeeper)

> "Hi, this is Alex with Calldesk. I'm calling a few insurance agencies in the Tampa area about a quick question — does [Agency Name] ever miss calls after hours or when everyone's on the phone?"

## If they say YES (interested):

> "Got it. Most agencies I talk to say the same thing — a call at 7 PM or during lunch is usually a new lead that goes to voicemail. We built an AI phone agent that answers those calls, qualifies the lead, and books an appointment right on your calendar. It sounds like a real person, not a robot. Worth a 2-minute demo this week? I can show you how it works with a live call."

## If they say NO (not interested):

> "No problem — if that ever changes, feel free to reach out. We're at calldesk.tech. Have a good one."

## If they say "TELL ME MORE" (hooked):

> "Happy to. The agent picks up in under 2 seconds, greets the caller by your agency name, asks what they're looking for — home, auto, life — and gets their name, number, and best time for a callback. It can even pull a quote if you connect your CRM. Takes 10 minutes to set up. What does your after-hours look like right now — straight to voicemail?"

## Objection: "We already have a service"

> "Makes sense — who's handling it? [Pause] The difference with ours is it's trained on YOUR specific policies and phrasing, not a generic script. Plus most answering services charge per minute — ours is a flat monthly. When does your current contract come up?"

## Objection: "Too expensive"

> "Fair concern. What are you paying for after-hours coverage now? [Let them answer] We typically come in at about half that for 24/7 answering. And the first missed call that turns into a policy pays for the whole year. Want me to send over a quick pricing breakdown?"

## Objection: "Just email me something"

> "Sure — what's the best email? [Write it down] I'll send a 30-second demo video and our pricing. Can I follow up Thursday to see what you think?"

## Objection: "Send me info and I'll call you back"

> "I'd love to, but honestly — do you ever call back salespeople? [Light laugh] What I CAN do is send you a 30-second demo of our AI actually answering a call. If it sounds interesting, I'll call you Friday. If not, I'll never bug you again. Fair?"

## Objection: "We're not looking right now"

> "Totally understand — most agencies aren't until they realize how many leads they're losing. Quick question: do you know what percentage of your after-hours calls are new prospects vs existing clients?"

## Objection: "We use voicemail and it works fine"

> "Got it. How many voicemails do you get per week? [Let them answer] And of those, how many do you actually convert? The agencies we work with were shocked — they thought voicemail was fine until they saw 60% of callers hang up without leaving one. The AI captures 100% of them."

## Closing (always ask for SOMETHING):

- **Best:** "Can we do a 10-minute demo Thursday at 2 or Friday at 10?"
- **Good:** "I'll send the demo video — can I call you back Friday to get your thoughts?"
- **Minimum:** "I'll add you to our follow-up list — is quarterly updates okay or too much?"

## Calldesk Advantages to Mention

1. **Speed**: Picks up in under 2 seconds (faster than voicemail)
2. **Custom**: Trained on YOUR agency's policies and phrasing
3. **Action**: Books appointments, takes messages, transfers urgent calls
4. **Price**: Flat monthly, cheaper than most answering services
5. **Setup**: 10 minutes, no IT team needed
6. **Proof**: *"We've already benchmarked against competitors and published the results — want to see?"*

## Voice & Tone Rules

- **Sound like a peer**, not a vendor. "I'm calling a few agencies in your area" > "I'd like to tell you about our product"
- **Ask questions, don't pitch.** Every sentence should end with a question or pause for them to talk.
- **Be brief.** If you talk for more than 20 seconds without a pause, you've lost them.
- **Use their name** twice in the first minute.
- **Never apologize** for calling: "Sorry to bother you" sounds needy. "Quick question" sounds confident.
- **If they say no, leave gracefully.** A polite "no" today is better than an annoyed "never call me again."

## Metrics to Track

- **Connection rate**: How many pick up / how many dialed
- **Interest rate**: How many say "tell me more" / how many connected
- **Demo scheduled**: How many book a demo / how many interested
- **Average call duration**: Track how long profitable calls last
- **Best time to call**: Note what time of day gets the most pickups

## Follow-up Cadence (for leads who don't pick up)

- **Day 1**: Call at 10 AM
- **Day 2**: Call at 2 PM (different time)
- **Day 3**: Text + call at 4 PM
- **Day 7**: Email + call
- **Day 14**: Final call, then mark as " nurture" for quarterly check-in
